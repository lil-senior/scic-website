Programme Details

What to expect during your fellowship

🫀

Core Clinical Competencies

  • Cardiomyopathy (HCM, DCM, ARVC)
  • Myocarditis & inflammatory disease
  • Ischemia, viability & stress perfusion
  • Congenital heart disease
  • Valvular & pericardial disease
  • Cardiac masses & oncology
⚙️

Technical Training

  • Cardiac MR physics fundamentals
  • Pulse sequence optimization
  • T1/T2/T2* mapping techniques
  • 4D flow acquisition
  • AI-assisted post-processing
  • Stress perfusion protocols
🎓

Academic Development

  • Weekly didactic teaching series
  • Journal club (biweekly)
  • Case conference presentations
  • Conference attendance support
  • Manuscript preparation mentorship
  • Oral presentation coaching

Fellows are paired with a research mentor at the start of the year to develop a structured research project, with the goal of completing a manuscript-ready study by year-end.

Months 1–3

Literature review, hypothesis generation, study design, ethics application.

Months 4–9

Data collection, analysis, and results interpretation with supervisor guidance.

Months 10–12

Manuscript writing, abstract submission to major conference, journal submission.

How to Apply

Applications for the 2026–2027 fellowship are open

We welcome applications from cardiologists who have completed or are near completion of a general cardiology fellowship and wish to specialize in cardiac MRI. International applicants welcome.

📋
CV and personal statement (max 1 page explaining your interest in cardiac MRI and career goals)
✉️
