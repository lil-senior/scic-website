About SCIC

Two Decades of Cardiac Imaging Leadership

Founded at the University of Calgary, the Stephenson Cardiac Imaging Centre has grown from a regional resource into an internationally recognized centre of excellence.

History Our Team
Our History

Building a legacy of innovation

Named in honour of Mr. Ken Stephenson, whose generous philanthropic vision made this centre possible and continues to inspire our mission.

2005

Foundation

Founded at the Foothills Hospital as a collaborative effort between a group of visionary philanthropists, Alberta Health Services, and the Divisions of Cardiology and Diagnostic Imaging.

2005-2013

Growth & Recognition under Dr. Matthias Friedrich

Clinical volumes grew rapidly through the efforts of the cardiologist and cardiac imager recruited from Germany. This program was the first of its kind in Canada and established as an important pillar in (Southern) Alberta.

2013-2025

Precision Medicine by Dr. James Alexander White

The transition brought a new academic vision and strengthened the existing team. With the academic focus aimed towards validating the strengths of CMR to prevent sudden cardiac deaths, the SCIC was well posed to guide the practical use of this imaging modality in routine patient care.

2025 and continuing

Expanding Innovation and Translation by Dr. Marco Götte

Dr. Marco Götte introduced a new research program: interventional CMR, advanced tissue characterization, electrophysiology integration, and quantitative cardiovascular imaging, while continuing its established commitment to clinical CMR excellence and education.
