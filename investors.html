Technology Ecosystem

Our innovation pipeline

From acquisition to analysis to intervention — SCIC's research spans the full cardiac MRI technology stack.

Pulse Sequence Innovation

5D cine, ultra-fast real-time, gadolinium-free protocols — advancing what the scanner can capture in less time.

AI & Machine Learning

Automated segmentation, radiomic feature extraction, and predictive analytics trained on 20+ years of clinical data.

Interventional CMR Devices

MRI-compatible catheters, real-time tracking, and ablation visualization systems for the cardiac catheterization lab of the future.

ECG Signal Processing

MHD artefact correction, MR-compatible 12-lead ECG, and ECGi integration — enabling diagnostic-quality cardiac electrophysiology inside the scanner.

Clinical Workflow Optimization

Standardized protocols, automated reporting, and referral intelligence to maximize throughput, reduce costs, and increase diagnostic yield.

Tissue Characterization

Novel MRI biomarkers for fibrosis, inflammation, and amyloid — non-invasive diagnostics reducing the need for biopsy and enabling precision medicine.
