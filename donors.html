Your Generosity Powers Discovery

Philanthropic support is essential to our mission. Every donation — large or small — enables us to push the boundaries of cardiac imaging, train the next generation of leaders, and improve outcomes for patients with heart disease.

Impact

Where your investment goes

Donations to SCIC directly fund the work that changes lives — from scanner upgrades to student stipends to groundbreaking research.

🔬

Research Excellence

Fund novel research programmes, graduate student stipends, and access to the latest analysis tools that go beyond what grants alone can cover.

🧲

Technology & Innovation

Support acquisition of next-generation MRI equipment, advanced coils, and hardware upgrades that keep SCIC at the frontier of cardiac imaging.

🎓

Education & Training

Support named fellowships, travel awards for trainees, and educational programmes that train the cardiac imagers of tomorrow.
