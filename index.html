What we do

A centre built on three pillars

Clinical care, research, and education form the foundation of the Stephenson Cardiac Imaging Centre (SCIC). Across each pillar is a shared commitment to excellence, collaboration, and improving cardiovascular care.

Clinical Care

Delivering comprehensive cardiovascular MRI for patients across Southern Alberta.

Research

Advancing cardiovascular imaging through translational research in tissue characterization, electrophysiology, quantitative imaging, and MRI-guided intervention.
